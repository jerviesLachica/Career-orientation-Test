SHS Strands Overview

🔬

STEM

Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics

Perfect for students who love problem-solving, innovation, and discovery. Ideal for future engineers, scientists, and tech professionals.

  • Engineering
  • Computer Science
  • Medicine
  • Architecture
💼

ABM

Accountancy, Business, Management

Designed for students interested in business, finance, and leadership. Great for future entrepreneurs and business professionals.

  • Accounting
  • Business Management
  • Marketing
  • Entrepreneurship
📚

HUMSS

Humanities and Social Sciences

For students passionate about society, culture, and human behavior. Perfect for future teachers, lawyers, and social workers.

  • Law
  • Education
  • Psychology
  • Social Work
🎓

GAS

General Academic Strand

A flexible option for students who haven't decided yet or want a broader academic foundation. Allows exploration of multiple fields.

  • Any bachelor's degree
  • Flexible career options
  • Further specialization
🔧

TVL

Technical-Vocational-Livelihood

For hands-on learners who want practical skills and immediate job opportunities. Perfect for trades and vocational careers.

  • Automotive Technology
  • Hospitality
  • Construction
  • Electronics
